## Our model
- SLOT Wellness partners with your community to implent a wellness program that is built into the residents monthly expenses.
- The facility incorporates the wellness program into its monthly resident pricing structure.
- This adjustment aligns with the value of enhanced programming and lifestyle offersing provided.
- The facility then remits the agreed-upon portion to SLOT Wellness on a monthly basis.
- This model keeps billing simple, centeralized, and seamless for residents and management.

## helps you!
- Your facility receives a **guaranteed monthly stipend** paid to your community by SLOT Wellness, as part of our partnership agreement.
- SLOT Wellness provides **consistent, predictable revenue** with no added workload to you and your team.
- No financial risk to your facility: no upfront investment required, no staffing cost added to your payroll and no disruption to your current therapy or care sevices.
