## Our Program

Our program focuses on supporting residents in maintaining their abilities , preventing decline, and improving overall quality of life. Rather than waiting for problems to arise, SLOT Wellness emphasizes prevention, maintenance, restoration and helping residents stay active and engaged in their daily lives.

Through personalized wellness sessions , residents can work on areas such as but not limited to: strength, balance, and mobility, cognitive wellness and memory support, language enhancement through social interaction, nutrition and hydration maintenance, vocal hygiene, and maintaining independence with everyday activities Our team works closely with residents and staff to provide individualized and group wellness support in a comfortable and familiar environment.

SLOT Wellness is designed to be simple, accessible, and resident-centered. Residents enroll directly in the program and receive ongoing wellness services in a way that fits their personal goals and lifestyle.

## Benefits

- Your community receives a **guaranteed monthly stipend** paid to your community by SLOT Wellness, as part of our partnership agreement.
- SLOT Wellness provides **consistent, predictable revenue** with no added workload to you and your team.
- No financial risk to your community: no upfront investment required, no staffing cost added to your payroll and no disruption to your current therapy or care sevices.
