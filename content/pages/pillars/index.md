summary = 'We promote enrichment, vitality, independence and connection'

- Life Enrichment: Providing opportunities that promote purpose, joy, and fulfillment.
- Inclusion: Celebrating diversity and fostering belonging in every activity and interaction.
- Vitality: Encouraging movement, energy, and a healthy lifestyle.
- Engagement: Encouraging active participation and meaningful involvement in daily life.
- Whole-Person Wellness: Supporting physical, cognitive, emotional, and social well-being.
- Empowered Independence: Promoting dignity, autonomy, and confidence by helping individuals maintain independence while aging with purpose and choice.
- Lifelong Connection: Believing meaningful relationships and social engagement are essential to wellness at every stage of life.
- Living with Integrity: Operating with honesty, transparency, and ethical clarity, staying true to wellness-focused, community-centered care.
