## Welcome to The SLOT Wellness Family

Our mission is to promote whole person wellness by integrating movement, nutrition, social connection, cognitive stimulation, and emotional support while aging with dignity and purpose. Our vision is to be a trusted partner in a vibrant community where wellness is woven into daily life — enhancing quality of life, promoting functional independence, and supporting optimal health outcomes through movement, meaningful connections, and well being at every stage of life. We promote our mission and vision through our **LIVE WELL** Pillars.

Our program focuses on supporting residents in maintaining their abilities, preventing decline, and improving overall quality of life. Rather than waiting for problems to arise, SLOT Wellness emphasizes prevention, maintenance, restoration and helping residents stay active and engaged in their daily lives.

Through personalized wellness sessions, residents can work on areas such as but not limited to: strength, balance, and mobility, cognitive wellness and memory support, language enhancement through social interaction, nutrition and hydration maintenance, vocal hygiene, and maintaining independence with everyday activities

Our team works closely with residents and staff to provide individualized and group wellness support in a comfortable and familiar environment.

SLOT Wellness is designed to be simple, accessible, and resident centered. Residents enroll directly in the program and receive ongoing wellness services in a way that fits their personal goals and lifestyle.

We are excited to be part of your community and look forward to supporting the health, independence, and well-being of your residents. If you have any questions or would like to learn more about the program, please feel free to reach out.
